How Hiro works

From the wider market to the opportunities that matter.

Hiro is not a tender-alert service. It structures the market, scores it around your organisation and helps focus effort where it matters.

L0

Data

Structure. Standardise. Enrich.

Downloadable, CRM-ready procurement intelligence built from fragmented public-sector sources and normalised into a consistent market view.

L1

Profile

You create the profile. Hiro scores the market.

Profiles can reflect services, sectors, buyers, geography, exclusions and value. Hiro scores opportunities against that context so the market is organised around you.

L2

Advanced Intelligence

Fit. Confidence. Risk. Rationale.

AI-assisted decision intelligence supports Pursue, Watch or Decline choices. It is decision support, not autopilot.
